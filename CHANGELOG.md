# Changelog — Project Gambit

All notable development milestones and public releases are documented in this file.

## [v0.6] — Boost & High-RPM Simulation Verification
- Implemented 13 PSIG boosted charge simulation model.
- Evaluated high-RPM volumetric efficiency alignment between intake runners and exhaust primaries.
- Simulated transmission coupling and dynamic torque transfer.

## [v0.5] — High-RPM NA Powerband Calibration
- Optimized natural aspiration gas dynamics for 10,000–13,000 RPM operation.
- Validated multi-angle cam event timing and high-speed combustion turbulence limits.
- Conducted baseline dyno sweep evaluations.

## [v0.4] — Multi-Bank Quad E-Turbo Architecture
- Formulated distributed 4-bank forced induction proxies (8 psi and 13 psi configurations).
- Analyzed intercooled charge density and exhaust backpressure behavior.
- Documented thermal constraints under sustained maximum load.

## [v0.3] — Thermal Capsule & Distributed Dry Sump
- Established distributed dry-sump lubrication principles for 4-bank geometry.
- Defined active scavenging criteria to eliminate oil pooling under high-G cornering.
- Formulated external thermal jacket architecture for multi-circuit cooling.

## [v0.2] — Hyper-GT Concept & Packaging Doctrine
- Formalized the non-negotiable 4-adult-occupant cabin architecture.
- Established vehicle character, styling doctrine, and driving modes (Road, Sport, Track).
- Initiated virtual prototyping strategy with multi-body physics tools.

## [v0.1] — Kinematic Proof-of-Concept
- Validated basic 4-bank kinematics and single-degree-of-freedom constraint networks.
- Resolved inter-cylinder bank collisions through station pitch optimization.
