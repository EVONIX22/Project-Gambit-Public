# Tribological Validation Statement

## Hydrodynamic Lubrication & Bearing Survivability

### Summary Finding
Numerical evaluations of the cranktrain interfaces under simulated cyclic firing pressures (up to 13,000 RPM) demonstrate that:

> **"Full 2D Reynolds transient hydrodynamic evaluation completed without solid contact across the tested high-load operating regimes."**

### Engineering Highlights
- **Reciprocating Load Accommodation:** The bearing interfaces maintain continuous hydrodynamic fluid film under alternating combustion and inertia loads.
- **Fluid Film Integrity:** Transient squeeze and wedge pressures remain sufficient to prevent cold welding (*galling*) when supplied with designated synthetic lubricants under regulated pressure.
- **Design Safeguards:** Oil supply galleries are isolated from gravity dependence through distributed pressure manifolds.
