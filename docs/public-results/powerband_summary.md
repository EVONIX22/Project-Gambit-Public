# Powertrain Simulation & Powerband Summary

## Executive Summary
Simulations conducted across natural aspiration (NA) and forced-induction configurations demonstrate the potential of the high-RPM compact X-engine architecture.

## Operating Regimes Evaluated

### 1. Naturally Aspirated Baseline (v0.5 NA)
- **Displacement:** ~4.0 Liters.
- **Redline:** 13,000 RPM.
- **Tuned Acoustic Resonance:** Dual intake/exhaust resonance aligned at ~11,400 RPM.
- **Observed Peak Output:** ~430–440 HP in simulation holding stable BMEP boundaries (~13.8–14.0 bar).

### 2. Boosted Proxy (v0.4.1 @ 8 PSIG)
- **Manifold Absolute Pressure:** ~22.7 psia.
- **Charge Air Density:** ~1.47x ambient (intercooled).
- **Observed Peak Output:** ~560 HP.

### 3. High-Boost Performance Calibration (v0.6 @ 13 PSIG)
- **Manifold Absolute Pressure:** ~27.7 psia.
- **Charge Air Density:** ~1.75x ambient (intercooled).
- **Observed Peak Output:** ~657 HP at 10,000 RPM.
- **Combustion Stability:** Maintained within calculated knock-margin limits under retarded ignition mapping.

> *Note: Exact crankpin indexing, valve lift profiles, and firing orders are proprietary and omitted.*
