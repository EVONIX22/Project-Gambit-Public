# Vehicle Architecture & Systems Concept

## 1. Platform Philosophy
Project Gambit explores a luxury Hyper-GT architecture designed around four full-size adult occupants. Unlike conventional two-seat supercars, the chassis packaging balances a high-cylinder-count powertrain with uncompromising cabin comfort, high torsional rigidity, and functional aerodynamics.

## 2. Powertrain Integration
- **Engine Configuration:** 4-bank X-architecture (X8 / X16 platforms).
- **Induction:** Distributed quad electric-assisted turbocharging (one turbo group per bank).
- **Lubrication:** Distributed dry sump with active scavenge per bank.
- **Cooling:** Multi-circuit thermal management with an auxiliary thermal buffer.

## 3. Operational Driving Modes
1. **Road Mode:** Tamed throttle response, quiet active exhaust, maximum cabin acoustic isolation, compliant adaptive suspension.
2. **Sport Mode:** Sharper power delivery, dynamic suspension firming, enhanced stability thresholds.
3. **Track Mode:** Uncapped output, pre-conditioned battery and charge temperatures, maximum aerodynamic downforce, full telemetry acquisition.
