# Engineering Roadmap & Virtual Prototyping Stages

## Virtual-First Methodology
To minimize material waste and de-risk radical mechanical concepts, Project Gambit follows a rigorous virtual development pipeline:

```text
Requirements Definition
        ↓
Parametric CAD Kinematics
        ↓
Multi-Body Collision & Clearance Sweeps
        ↓
Dynamic Load & Tribological Modeling
        ↓
Engine Gas-Dynamics & Acoustic Simulation
        ↓
Physical Subsystem Test Benches
        ↓
Full Experimental Powertrain
```

## Progressive Learning Stages
1. **Kinematic Verification:** 1-DOF constraint network validation across 0–1080° crank revolutions.
2. **Tribological Validation:** Fluid film retention under high reciprocating loads.
3. **Gas Dynamics & Boost Modeling:** Acoustic intake tuning and manifold pressure modeling.
4. **Thermal System Prototyping:** Distributed cooling and lubrication flow rigs.
5. **Physical Hardware Machining:** Precision CNC manufacturing of validation assemblies.
